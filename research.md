# Roti Count Tracker for Jhelum Mess, NIT Srinagar

## The problem (5 lines)
1. Shop/Location: Jhelum Mess, inside NIT Srinagar campus.
2. What I saw: I noticed that estimating the right number of rotis for each meal can be challenging, sometimes resulting in shortages or leftovers.
3. Why it matters: Preparing too few rotis can cause shortages, while preparing too many leads to food wastage.
4. What the staff currently does: The daily roti requirement is handled by the mess staff. I will discuss their current estimation process with them.
5. What my tool will do: Use actual daily roti preparation and consumption records to help estimate how many rotis should be prepared for upcoming meals.

## My person
- **Butler:** Abdul Qayoom
- **Cook:** Mohammad Mukhtar
- **Location:** Jhelum Mess, NIT Srinagar.

## Existing solutions (2) and why they don't fit
1. Manual registers: Can record daily quantities but don't automatically analyse consumption patterns or suggest preparation quantities.
2. General inventory management software: Usually designed for shops and businesses rather than the daily roti preparation needs of a college mess.
